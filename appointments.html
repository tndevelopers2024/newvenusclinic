Frequently Asked Questions

What does a cardiologist do? +
A cardiologist is a medical doctor who specializes in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of heart-related conditions and diseases. They focus on managing heart health and the circulatory system.
When should I see a cardiologist? +
You should consider seeing a cardiologist if you have symptoms like chest pain, shortness of breath, irregular heartbeat, high blood pressure, or a family history of heart disease. Regular check-ups are also recommended for preventive care.
What is a stress test, and why might I need one? +
A stress test, also known as an exercise stress test, helps assess how your heart responds to physical activity. It's used to detect heart problems, especially when symptoms like chest pain or shortness of breath occur during exercise.
What is an echocardiogram? +
An echocardiogram is an ultrasound of the heart. It creates images of the heart's structure and function, helping diagnose heart conditions, such as heart valve problems, heart muscle diseases, and more.
How can I lower my risk of heart disease? +
To reduce your risk of heart disease, maintain a healthy lifestyle. This includes regular exercise, a balanced diet, not smoking, managing stress, and controlling conditions like high blood pressure and diabetes. Your cardiologist can provide personalized guidance.
What is a pacemaker, and who needs one? +
A pacemaker is a device that helps regulate an irregular heartbeat by sending electrical signals to the heart. It's typically recommended for people with bradycardia (slow heart rate) or certain heart rhythm disorders.
What is an angioplasty and stent placement? +
Angioplasty is a procedure used to open narrowed or blocked blood vessels, usually in the coronary arteries. A stent is a tiny tube inserted to keep the vessel open. This is often done to improve blood flow to the heart muscle and treat coronary artery disease.
How can I make an appointment with a cardiologist? +
To make an appointment, you can call our office at or use our online booking system on our website. We recommend a referral from your primary care physician if you have one.
