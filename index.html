Medical Services

Our Healthcare Service

At New Venus Clinic, we are dedicated to providing the highest quality cardiovascular care to our patients. Our experienced and compassionate team of cardiologists is committed to helping you achieve and maintain a healthy heart.

CONSULTATION

We use cutting-edge diagnostic tools to identify and treat a wide range of heart conditions, from arrhythmias to coronary artery disease.

ECG

Dr. C.R. Madhu Prabhu Doss, a leading interventional cardiologist, performs life-saving procedures to treat heart conditions, such as blockages and stenosis.

ECHO [2D ECHO CARDIOGRAM]

Dr. C.R. Madhu Prabhu Doss specializes in managing hypertension and heart disease offering tailored treatment plans and guidance on lifestyle modifications to control high blood pressure and treatment of heart disease.

TMT

We provide comprehensive diabetes care, including education on managing the condition, medication management, and personalized treatment plans.

LAB SERVICES

Early detection is key, so we offer heart health screenings to assess your risk factors and recommend preventive measures.

PHARMACY

Our experienced team ensures that medication is prescribed appropriately and monitored for optimal results.
